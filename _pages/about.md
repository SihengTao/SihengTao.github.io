I am a master’s student in **Computational Biology** and Quantitative Genetics at Harvard University, working with [Dr. Bo Xia](https://www.boxialab.org/team/about-bo-xia) at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard.

I am broadly interested in how gene regulation shapes cell identity and how cells maintain or change their states, particularly in immune cells and brain cells. My research combines computational epigenomic analyses with deep learning models to explore how chromatin, transcription factors, and other regulatory mechanisms contribute to these processes. My long-term goal is to understand how cell fate is determined and how we can systematically regulate it.

Previously, I worked on microbiology with Dr. Chongde Wu and epidemiology with Dr. Xuelei Ma. I am grateful for these diverse research experiences, which continue to shape how I think.

Outside of research, I enjoy photography, traveling, and working out. Years of training in Mandarin debate have also taught me to value listening to different perspectives. I enjoy talking to people with different backgrounds and perspectives. Feel free to reach out!
